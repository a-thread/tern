Delete your Tern account

Tern, by a-thread.codes

In the app

  1. Open Tern and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Your data.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

The account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

What is deleted

Your login (email address and name), food log, saved meals, weigh-ins, waypoints, rest days and settings are permanently erased from Tern's database. Nothing is kept afterwards and it cannot be undone. Step counts are read from Health Connect on your device and are never uploaded, so there is nothing to delete for them.

Can't open the app?

Email aiden.threadgoode@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Tern account”, and your account will be deleted within 30 days.
