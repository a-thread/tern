Tern support

Help with Tern, from Purlieus LLC.

Get in touch

Email admin@purlieus.dev. Tell us which phone you use (iPhone or Android) and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Export your data

  1. Open Tern and go to Settings.
  2. Open Your data (Account and data when you're signed in).
  3. Tap Export my data.

You get a copy of everything Tern holds about you, as a JSON file. The file isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Delete your data or account

In Settings, under Your data (Account and data when you're signed in), tap Delete my data to erase your log and keep your login, or Delete my account to remove everything.
