Tern: A Kinder Tracker

Progress answers to your pace.

No bargaining with calories. No chasing a size. Just regular movement, and goals you choose.

Each day you show up, your Arctic tern travels a little further. Move when you can, log what sustains you, and always rest when you need to.

Who Tern is for

People tired of dieting apps that race to a number

Tern keeps sustainability in mind.

  • A rest day counts as showing up, so your streak carries right through it.
  • Walking part of the way is never counted as worse than not walking at all.
  • Nothing warns you for going over, and exercise never earns food back.
  • Follow the trend, so a single morning never decides how you feel.

People who've had a hard relationship with food, tracking or exercise

Tern was built with disordered eating, tracking and movement patterns in mind. That's why it's highly customizable.

  • Hide calorie counts whenever the numbers get in the way.
  • Turn weight tracking off and every reference to it goes away.
  • Turn food tracking off and keep a gentle reminder to eat, with nothing to log.
  • Foods get a NOVA processing level as information, never a judgment.
  • Every reminder is optional, goes quiet once you've done the thing, and never scolds.
  • Seeing a dietitian, therapist or doctor? Share a plain summary of the last 2 or 4 weeks, with only what you track and none of your notes.

Track what helps you meet your goals

  • Movement

    Set a daily step goal and connect Apple Health or Health Connect. Log workouts and how hard they felt.

  • Food Optional

    Track calories, macros or neither. Search everyday foods and packaged products, scan a barcode, or log the way you'd say it: a slice, a cup or a spoonful.

  • Weight Optional

    Track daily, weekly or not at all, and skip a weigh-in whenever you need to.

  • Water, mood and medication Optional

    A daily water goal, a quick mood and stress check-in, and medication reminders, each only if you want them.

  • Trends

    See the last 7 days, 30 days or 6 months of whatever you track.

  • A journey for showing up

    Your tern flies the route real Arctic terns were tracked flying: 26 stops and 71,200 km from Greenland down to the Weddell Sea and home again.

Your data is yours

  • No ads, nothing sold.
  • No account required: stay anonymous and keep everything on your phone, free.
  • Tern Plus is the one paid part: an account that keeps your log on every phone you sign in to, on a secure server.
  • Export everything and restore it on a new phone, or delete your data or your account any time, right from Settings.
