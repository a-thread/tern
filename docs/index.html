Tern privacy policy

Last updated: 9/19/2026

Tern is a personal health tracker for steps, food and weight. This page says what it stores, where, and what you can do about it.

What Tern stores

If you create an account, Tern stores, against your account:

  • your email address and first name (used to sign you in and greet you),
  • your food log (what you log, the meal, and its nutrition values) and the meals you save (named groups of foods),
  • your weigh-ins (stored in pounds, shown in pounds or kilograms as you choose),
  • your waypoints, rest days and settings (goals, units, reminder times),
  • if you choose to track water: each drink you log (an amount and a time), and your daily goal,
  • if you choose to track medication: the name and due time you give each one, and which days you marked it as taken. Nothing else about it, such as doses or why you take it.

This data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database, and is protected so that only your signed-in account can read or change it.

If you use the preview without an account, nothing is saved: the sample data disappears when you exit.

Steps and Health Connect

If you connect Health Connect on Android, Tern reads your step counts only, and only to show your steps, your streak and your daily goal. Step counts are read on your device. Tern does not upload them and does not write anything back to Health Connect. You can remove Tern's access at any time in Health Connect, and everything you have logged stays.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They don't go through a server, and you can turn them off in Settings.

What Tern does not do

  • It does not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.
  • It does not use your weight or calories to award anything. Waypoints come from showing up: reaching your step goal, logging your meals, taking a rest day.

Your choices

In Settings, under Your data, you can:

  • Export my data: get a copy of everything Tern holds about you (JSON).
  • Delete my data: erase your food log, saved meals, weigh-ins, water and medication history, waypoints, rest days and settings. Your login is kept. How to delete your data.
  • Delete my account: permanently delete your account and everything stored with it. See how to delete your account.

Food information

Nutrition information for packaged foods comes from Open Food Facts, used under the Open Database License, and for everyday foods from USDA FoodData Central (public domain). When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode number is sent to these public databases so it can be looked up. Nothing else about you or your account is sent with it, though, as with any web request, the services can see your IP address.

Camera

If you use the barcode scanner, Tern asks for camera access. The camera is used only to read the barcode on screen; nothing is recorded, photographed or saved.
