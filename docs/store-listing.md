# Google Play

## App name

Tern: A Kinder Tracker

## Short description (80 characters max)

Progress answers to your pace.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

Inspired by the Arctic tern, the bird with the longest migration on Earth, Tern is a tracker built for the long haul.

Each day you show up, your Arctic tern travels a little further along its migration. Move when you can, log what sustains you, and always rest when you need to.

No bargaining with calories. No chasing a size. Just regular movement, and goals you choose.

Track the things that help you meet your goals:

MOVEMENT
• Set a daily step goal and connect Health Connect. Your steps and workouts are read on your phone and never uploaded.
• Log workouts and how hard they felt.
• Take rest days that keep your momentum. A rest day counts as showing up, so your streak carries right through it.
• Walking part of the way is never counted as worse than not walking at all.
• Raise your goal whenever you're ready, or add rest days when you need a longer break.

FOOD (OPTIONAL)
• Track calories, macros or neither, and hide calorie counts whenever the numbers get in the way. Nothing warns you for going over, and exercise never earns food back.
• Foods get a NOVA processing level as information, never a judgment, and you decide whether to show NOVA numbers, colors or neither.
• Search everyday foods and packaged products, or scan a barcode.
• Log the way you'd say it: a packet, a slice, a cup or a spoonful, in ounces, pounds or grams, and drinks in fl oz or ml.
• Add your own foods, or cook a batch: log the whole pot once, say how many servings it makes, then log a bowl at a time.
• Save meals, or re-log yesterday's dinner in a tap.
• Didn't have lunch? Mark it "nothing today" and your log still counts as complete.
• Opt in to a quick "How was it?" after meals to see what sits well.
• Want a hand? Tern can learn how much energy you really use from your log and weight trend and suggest a calorie target. It never changes one on its own.
• Not counting at all? Turn food tracking off and keep a gentle reminder to eat, with nothing to log.

WEIGHT (OPTIONAL)
• Track daily, weekly or not at all. Turn weight off and every reference to it goes away.
• Skip a weigh-in whenever you need to.
• Gain, lose or hold steady: your log is there for whatever goal you set. Follow the trend, so a single morning never decides how you feel.
• Pounds or kilograms, your choice.

WATER (OPTIONAL)
• Set a daily goal and watch it fill up, with reminders on a schedule that suits you, or none at all.

MOOD, STRESS AND NOTES (OPTIONAL)
• A quick daily check-in to see how you're doing over time, and a few words about your day if you want them.

MEDICATION (OPTIONAL)
• Add as many as you need, each daily or weekly, with its own reminder if you want one.

TRENDS
• See the last 7 days, 30 days or 6 months of whatever you track, and tap any bar or point for its day and value.

A JOURNEY FOR SHOWING UP
• Your tern flies the route real Arctic terns were tracked flying: 26 stops and 71,200 km from Greenland down to the Weddell Sea and home again.
• Waypoints are earned by showing up for the habits you choose. Reach home and your tern sets off again, so the journey never ends.

GENTLE REMINDERS
• Every reminder is optional, goes quiet once you've done the thing, and never scolds.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• No ads, nothing sold.
• No account required: stay anonymous and keep everything on your phone, free.
• Tern Plus is the one paid part: an account that keeps your log on every phone you sign in to, on a secure server. Supporter is the same, with a thank-you.
• Export everything and restore it on a new phone, or delete your data or your account any time, right from Settings.
• Seeing a dietitian, therapist or doctor? Share a plain summary of the last 2 or 4 weeks, with only what you track and show and none of your notes. Nothing is uploaded.

Nutrition information comes from Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central.

Tern is a tracking tool, not medical advice. Talk to a health professional before changing your diet or exercise.
