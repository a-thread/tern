Delete your Tern account

Tern, by Purlieus LLC

In the app

  1. Open Tern and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Your data.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

The account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

What is deleted

Your login (email address and name), food log, saved meals, weigh-ins, waypoints, rest days, water, medication and mood history and settings are permanently erased from Tern's database. Nothing is kept afterwards and it cannot be undone. Step counts and workouts are read from Health Connect or Apple Health on your device and are never uploaded, so there is nothing to delete for them.

Want to erase your data but keep your login? See how to delete just your data.

Can't open the app?

Email admin@purlieus.dev from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Tern account”, and your account will be deleted within 30 days.
